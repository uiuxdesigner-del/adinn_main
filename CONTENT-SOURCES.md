## People and culture research (Nov 2026 pass)

adinn.com has six pages (sitemap: home, about-us, service, projects, careers, contact). The Careers page says "We don't just hire people. We invest in talent", lists six values, and mentions "constant learning and upskilling" and "balance fuels creativity". No page mentions food, snacks, farm produce, milk, yoga or wellbeing programmes. Web searches found nothing either. These items are therefore **not published**. They are held for the owner's clarification (ask Adinn what "non fertilized snakes" and "natural cow milk without any segregation fat" mean, and what is provided, to whom and how often).

## Benchmark notes (own-words summary)

- WPP: repeats one growth proposition; shows capability through work and insights; groups privacy, terms, cookies and accessibility in the footer; contacts under "About".
- Publicis Groupe: frames its offer as one connected network ("The power of one"); keeps Careers and History with Governance; separate responsibility and investor sections.
- dentsu: names a network of agency brands plus case studies; "Who we are" groups background and leadership; Contact Us leads to a form and office locations.
What this changed for Adinn: a single company proposition, connected capabilities shown through client situations, contact routes in one place with offices, legal pages grouped in the footer, and responsibility/people content published only when documented.
