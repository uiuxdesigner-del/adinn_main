## 1. Legally required (once the facts above are confirmed)

| Requirement | Basis | Status on this site |
|---|---|---|
| Publish a privacy policy on the website if the company collects personal information | IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, Rule 4 (secondary sources) | Draft page only; the prototype form sends nothing. A real policy is needed before the live form or recruitment collects anything. |
| Name a Grievance Officer and publish their name and contact | Same Rules, Rule 5(9) (secondary sources) | Not published. Needs a named person. |
| Notice and consent for digital personal data | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025, notified Nov 2025. Notice and consent duties are reported to apply about 18 months later (around May 2027); Consent Manager rules about 12 months after notification | Not yet applicable per reports; design the final notice for it. Confirm the dates with MeitY. |

## 3. Recommended for clarity and trust (not shown to be required)

- Registered name, registered office and CIN in the footer once verified.
- A named grievance and enquiry contact (also needed under 1).
- Accessibility: aim for WCAG 2.1 AA. The site uses semantic landmarks, visible focus, skip link, labelled forms. Not formally audited.
- Cookies: this prototype sets none and loads no third-party scripts or analytics, so no cookie banner is shown. If analytics, embeds or ad tags are added, review consent and notice then. No cookie page is added now.
- Image permissions: credits are kept in the footer and IMAGE-SOURCES.md. Replace with commissioned or licensed photos before launch.
- Brand names shown on Home need written permission from each brand.
- Terms of use and copyright notice: not legally required for this site; add only if counsel advises.

## 4. Not applicable to this prototype

Payments, accounts, user tracking, newsletters and anything collecting data are not present, so related notices are not shown.
