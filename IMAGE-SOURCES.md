## Limitations

- Replaced in the second pass: Amritsar hoardings (Punjab), the Express Avenue atrium and the Shoppers Stop storefront, with Chennai, Bangalore and Kerala images whose locations are stated by the source pages.
- Pexels and Unsplash blocked automated access from this environment, so every image is from Wikimedia Commons. Quality is amateur documentary rather than studio stock; consider replacing with commissioned or licensed professional photography.
- Not every Commons subject is in South India. Locations are as stated by each source page; no regional identity is inferred from people.
- Several photos are 8–16 years old and show older signage and equipment.
- Photos include identifiable members of the public. They are licensed for reuse, but consider replacing the team photo (`about`) and the street market photo (`hero`) before any public launch.
- The "digital" photo shows an older workstation; it is a placeholder for digital marketing.
- Brand names visible in photos (Shoppers Stop, Renault, Aircel and others) belong to their owners. Their appearance does not imply any relationship with Adinn.

## Third pass (photography review)

- Removed the casual group meetup photo, the dense street-market crowd and the dated workstation photo. The site now relies on streets, signage, production and building photography with no prominent identifiable people.
- **Not achieved:** polished photographs of Indian business professionals in offices and meeting rooms. Pexels and Unsplash block automated access here; Wikimedia Commons and Openverse have no suitable licensed images of unnamed professionals, and the Openverse results were named executives at conferences, which would wrongly imply endorsement. Supply licensed or commissioned photos and register them in `lib/images.ts`.

## Captions

Visible image captions were removed. Photographs are general-purpose illustrations: none shows Adinn staff, premises or completed projects. Required CC attribution stays in the footer under "Photo credits". Work records keep the visible "Illustrative example" label because they describe concepts, not real projects.

**Not done:** Freepik and similar sites need an account and per-asset licence terms (many require visible attribution) and cannot be downloaded automatically here; no image-generation tool is available in this environment. The existing Commons photographs were kept.
